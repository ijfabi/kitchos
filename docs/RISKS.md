| R-012 | ~~No till/drawer session ties cash to a person and drawer~~                                                                                                                                    | Resolved                     | ADR-0015: cash requires the actor's own open drawer session (service + DB trigger); card still gated by `orders.create`                                                                                          |
| R-020 | Blind cashiers can remove more cash than the drawer holds (no 422, to avoid an expected-cash oracle)                                                                                           | Low                          | Shows as a variance at the count, which needs approval over the threshold; review movement reports                                                                                                               |
| R-022 | Receipts are posted at standard cost; supplier price differences (PPV) and payables are not in the journal                                                                                     | Medium                       | Invoice matching with PPV posting (Phase 5); GRNI balance shows uninvoiced receipts meanwhile                                                                                                                    |
| R-023 | A stock movement written outside the application without its event is valued but never posted                                                                                                  | Low                          | By design the day then fails to reconcile; all app paths emit events in the same transaction (ADR-0016)                                                                                                          |
| R-024 | ~~Wasting a line in parts rounded per portion, leaving cents in COGS~~                                                                                                                         | Resolved                     | Milestone 14: consumed food is wasted at its share of the consumption value, and the last portion takes the remainder                                                                                            |
| R-025 | Crew see the cost of waste they record, so they can derive an ingredient's standard cost, though they hold neither `inventory.view` nor `reports.view`                                         | Low                          | Accepted for now; return a cost-free view to crew if costs become restricted                                                                                                                                     |
| R-026 | Waste of a line not yet consumed, followed by its normal consumption, takes the line's portions twice. This is taken to mean a replacement was made (ADR-0011), with no explicit record of one | Low                          | Stock counts correct it; needs a product decision on whether completing the task proves a replacement                                                                                                            |
| R-021 | Drawer sessions that cross midnight post float/close at the opening business date; per-day cash on hand does not net to zero                                                                   | Low                          | Business-day close (next) closes or rolls drawers at the cutoff                                                                                                                                                  |
| R-027 | An entry that reaches a closed day posts to the next open day, so that closed day's own reconciliation view then shows a difference                                                            | Low                          | Rare (settling window, backlog check); the receiving day warns about it, and the closed day's snapshot is unchanged                                                                                              |
| R-028 | ~~Who may see live cash figures while their own till is open~~                                                                                                                                 | Accepted (product decision)  | Department managers see live figures at any time. Consequence: a manager running a till can work out its expected cash, so their own count is not blind; a different person still approves their variance        |
| R-029 | A date before the restaurant existed can be closed (harmless empty row), and moving a cutoff or timezone can make a closed day current again                                                   | Low                          | No settings API exists; entries then post to the next open day. A trading-start date would bound closes                                                                                                          |
| R-030 | ~~Reconciliation compared totals, so offsetting errors between tenders reconciled~~                                                                                                            | Resolved                     | Milestone 13: each tender, cash rounding, and every receipt and refund entry against its own source                                                                                                              |
| R-033 | Reconciliation checks only the tender lines inside receipt and refund entries; a wrong Card clearing or Cash on hand line under another entry type is not compared with a source               | Low                          | Drawer postings are reconciled through drawer variance; extend source matching to drawer entries if needed                                                                                                       |
| R-031 | ~~Food cost showed only food produced, not the recipe cost of what was sold~~                                                                                                                  | Resolved                     | Milestone 16: recipe cost of sales (theoretical food cost) beside actual, dated by the journal's sale and refund entries; unsold production shows the rest. Refunds by amount name no items and do not reduce it |
| R-032 | ~~Cancel after preparation left the food in COGS rather than waste~~                                                                                                                           | Resolved                     | Milestone 14: recorded as waste on cancel, adjustable                                                                                                                                                            |

| R-035 | ~~A small quality refund recorded every portion as waste~~                                                                                                                                     | Resolved (product decision)  | Milestone 15: refunds are by item, for the whole price of the item, which counts as waste; a replaced item counts as waste too. Refunds by amount are money only                                                 |
| R-036 | After a refund by amount, the remaining items may not all be refundable by item (the payment runs out first), and an item can be compensated by amount and then refunded in full               | Low                          | The screen explains the limit; a cap or a block is a product decision                                                                                                                                            |
| R-037 | refund_lines ties a line to a refund only in the service (under the order lock), not by a database constraint on the order                                                                     | Low                          | Composite keys on (order_id) would enforce it in the database                                                                                                                                                    |
| R-038 | Food cost per menu item cannot place refunds by amount on any item, so item rows overstate net sales by them; they are shown apart and the totals include them                                 | Low                          | Refunds by item are the default (Milestone 15); refunds by amount are for money-only cases                                                                                                                       |
| R-039 | ~~Rows in flight at a shift close (begun before, committed after) fell into no shift's snapshot~~                                                                                              | Resolved                     | Found by the Milestone 18 review and tester before release: rows are now stamped with their shift under the shift lock (ADR-0018), so a close waits for every row in its shift                                   |
